# 04 — Finding 1: The Thirteen Days

This is the most significant item in the record, and it is a matter of dates rather than opinion.

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **11 Sep 2026** | Acquisition completed. Consideration paid; 12,278,340 Class A shares issued. |
| **22 Sep 2026** | Form F-3 shelf registration filed — up to US$50 million. |
| **24 Sep 2026** | Business update. Prospective customers have all refused or indefinitely suspended cooperation. No confirmed customer orders or contracted revenue. Impairment of goodwill and intangibles under evaluation. |

**FACT** Thirteen days separate completion from that disclosure.

**INFERENCE** Commercial discussions with multiple counterparties do not ordinarily collapse in aggregate within thirteen days of an unrelated event. Two readings are available:

| Reading A — the position deteriorated after closing | Reading B — the position was substantially known at closing |
|---|---|
| If so, the company should be able to identify the external change, the date it occurred, and why it was not foreseeable at completion. | If so, the question becomes what diligence established, what the board was told, and whether disclosure at completion was complete. |

We express no view as to which applies.

> **A point of reconciliation.** The company's own draft registration statement, prepared in the same period, describes ZentoAI as one of its "key operating subsidiaries" through which the group conducts "sales of fintech products and services" — language difficult to reconcile with a subsidiary having no orders and no contracted revenue.

**QUESTION** This is the question a holder should put to the company.

# 05 — Finding 2: Impairment Exposure, Quantified

The company has said impairment is under evaluation. The scale is calculable from the filed terms.

| Component | Amount |
|---|---|
| Cash consideration — HKD 10,000,000 | approx. US$1,282,051 |
| Share consideration — 12,278,340 shares at US$0.476 | US$5,844,490 |
| **Total consideration** | **approx. US$7,126,541** |
| As a multiple of approximate annual revenue (US$3.16M) | 2.26× |

**FACT** ZentoAI was loss-making before acquisition — US$72,724 in FY2025 and US$21,733 in FY2024 on the company's own figures. The acquisition rationale therefore rested on prospects, not earnings.

**INFERENCE** Where the acquired prospects are stated to have gone, the goodwill and intangibles recognised on those prospects are exposed.

> A write-down consumes no cash, but it does two things that matter. It reduces net assets, which bears on Nasdaq's stockholders'-equity continued-listing standard (§08). And it establishes on the public record that shares were issued for an asset that did not deliver. The 12,278,340 shares are already issued — the dilution is permanent whether or not the asset is written down.

# 07 — Finding 4: Dilution Capacity — and Why US$50 Million Overstates It

> This section cuts against the bearish reading, and is included for that reason. The widely reported framing — a US$50 million shelf against US$3.16 million of revenue — is arithmetically correct but materially overstates near-term issuance capacity. A holder acting on the headline alone would be acting on an incomplete picture.

**Constraint 1 — the Nasdaq 19.99% limit**

**FACT** Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(d) requires shareholder approval before a listed company issues 20% or more of its shares outstanding at below the applicable minimum price. 19.99% of 17,719,499 Class A shares is 3,542,127 shares.

| Share price | Issuable without shareholder approval |
|---|---|
| US$1.20 | approx. US$4,250,552 |
| US$1.50 | approx. US$5,313,190 |

**Constraint 2 — the "baby shelf" limitation**

**FACT** General Instruction I.B.5 to Form F-3 restricts a company whose public float is below US$75 million to primary offerings of no more than one-third of that float in any twelve-month period.

**INFERENCE** The float has not been publicly quantified in the material reviewed. But on 17,719,499 Class A shares at US$1.20–1.50, total Class A market value is approximately US$21–27 million before excluding affiliate holdings. Float is therefore materially below US$75 million and the limitation appears to apply.

> **Conclusion on dilution.** US$50 million is a registration ceiling, not a capacity. Real near-term capacity is a small fraction of it. Dilution is a genuine risk on a multi-year view, and the company has signalled a clear intention to raise — but a holder should not assume US$50 million of stock is about to arrive.

# 08 — Finding 5: Listing Risk

Two continued-listing standards warrant monitoring, and they interact.

| Standard | Position |
|---|---|
| Minimum bid price — Nasdaq Rule 5550(a)(2) | Requires a US$1.00 closing bid. Shares have reportedly traded US$1.20–1.50 after a spike to US$2.35 and a prior period below US$0.70. The margin above the threshold is not wide. |
| Stockholders' equity | A material impairment of ZentoAI goodwill and intangibles reduces net assets and moves the company toward, not away from, the equity standard. |

> The combination is the risk. A write-down that pressures equity, in a stock already close to the bid threshold, is what produces a deficiency notice.

# 10 — Two Unfiled Claims — What They Would Mean, and How to Check

Two claims are circulating about ZTG. A holder will encounter them, so this section sets out exactly what is and is not on the record, what would follow arithmetically if either proved true, and how to verify them without relying on anyone's report, including this one.

> **Filing status as at 25 September 2026:** neither claim appears anywhere in the SEC filing record. No Form S-8 has been located. No PIPE has been filed, announced or disclosed. The sole origin of both is an extract from an AI-generated search conversation of unestablished provenance.

| Claim | What is asserted | Filing status |
|---|---|---|
| Form S-8 | Registration of approximately 20% of shares under an employee incentive plan | No filing located |
| US$6M PIPE | A private placement to related parties at a discount | No filing, no terms, no participants, no pricing |

**INFERENCE** We draw no conclusion from either claim and they support no finding in this report. We make no allegation that any officer, director or shareholder of the company has acted improperly. What follows is conditional arithmetic, provided so that a holder can evaluate a filing on the day it appears rather than reacting to a headline about it.

**If a Form S-8 were filed at the scale claimed**

**FACT** A Form S-8 registers shares issuable under employee benefit plans. It becomes effective on filing, and shares issued under it to non-affiliates are generally freely tradable without a resale holding period. It does not require shareholder approval, and it is not subject to the Nasdaq 19.99% limit.

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| 20% of 24,087,179 shares outstanding | 4,817,435 shares |
| As a proportion of the listed Class A class | 27.2% |

**INFERENCE** The significance is not the registration itself — incentive plans are ordinary. It is the combination of scale and the absence of a resale restriction. A plan of that size in a company of this float would be unusual, and is the specific thing to look at if a filing appears.

**If a US$6 million PIPE were priced at a discount**

**INFERENCE** Share counts at illustrative discounts to a US$1.20–1.50 market price:

| Market price | 10% disc. | 20% disc. | 30% disc. | 40% disc. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US$1.20 | 5,555,556 | 6,250,000 | 7,142,857 | 8,333,333 |
| US$1.50 | 4,444,444 | 5,000,000 | 5,714,286 | 6,666,667 |

> **The structural point a holder should take from this table.** The Nasdaq 19.99% threshold is 3,542,127 shares. A US$6 million placement at any discount in this price range produces 1.4× to 2.4× that number. It follows that a placement of that size could not be completed without shareholder approval under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5635(d) — or without being priced at or above the Nasdaq Minimum Price, which is the opposite of a discount.
>
> That is a meaningful constraint, and it cuts against the claim as described. It also tells a holder precisely what to look for: a notice of general meeting would have to appear before any such placement could close at a discount.

**Combined, if both occurred**

**INFERENCE** An S-8 of 4,817,435 shares plus a PIPE at the widest illustrated discount would total approximately 13.15 million shares — about 74% of the current Class A class. We stress that this is arithmetic on an unfiled hypothesis, not a forecast, and that the Nasdaq constraint above applies to the PIPE limb.

**How to verify this yourself — five minutes on EDGAR**

1. Go to sec.gov/edgar/search and search the company name or CIK. Do not rely on a secondary site.
2. Filter by form type S-8 and F-1 / F-3 / 424B. An S-8 appears immediately on filing; there is no delay.
3. For a PIPE, look for a Form 6-K describing a securities purchase agreement, and for a subsequent resale registration statement registering the investor's shares. A PIPE that has closed will leave both.
4. Check for a notice of general meeting furnished on Form 6-K. Per the note above, a discounted placement of this size requires shareholder approval, so the meeting notice would precede it.
5. Set an EDGAR email alert on the company's CIK. New filings arrive the same day, before any commentary about them.

> **A holder's position on unfiled claims.** Neither claim can be acted on today, because neither exists on the record today. Both can be checked in minutes, and would be visible to any holder at the same moment as to anyone else. The correct action is to set the alert and wait for the filing — not to trade on a report, including this one, that describes a document nobody has produced.

Where the publisher holds, or intends to take, a position in the security, it may also be characterised as market manipulation under Exchange Act sections 9(a) and 10(b).
